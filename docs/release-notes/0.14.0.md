This is an early release. Please report what you hit and attach the log (F1 > System > Log).

**You need your own disc image:** Metroid Prime 2: Echoes USA (`G2ME01`), PAL (`G2MP01`), or Trilogy (`R3ME01` USA, `R3MP01` PAL). No disc image or game assets are included.

## What changed

- **Fast elevator and light beam cutscenes** (F1 > Enhancements > Pace): the in-room departure and arrival cutscenes run at double speed. #86
- **The port's prompts are drawn by the game:** the skip prompt, the resume Yes/No, the "Press F1 for settings" hint and the shader count use the game's own boxes and text. Thanks to rivyr. #113
- **Sharper button icons:** the boxes' button disc and the main menu's selection orb draw each symbol from art made for it, the Lore scan's Start disc draws as a disc, and the ring fades with its box. Thanks to rivyr. #112 #114 #118
- **Saves on a card with an empty slot ahead of a full one** read right in the F1 save pages and the port's tools. Thanks to rivyr. #115
- **The quick save-state slot is kept between launches.** Thanks to rivyr. #116
- **Save states:** the Lightbringer's light zone no longer stays behind after a load or quick resume. #108
- **Import a Wii Trilogy save** (F1 > System > Memory card > Import file...): a `save.bin` from Dolphin's NAND brings its Echoes file into the card's save, backed up first.
- **Rezbit virus reboot:** RT works with Twin Stick Aim, and the presets with R on the right stick click show and accept it in the L+R+B reboot. #111
- **Hand on cannon:** fidgets wait while the left hand rests on the cannon. #117
- **Android:** under low memory, enemies that leave the view for a moment no longer vanish while their shaders rebuild.
- **The log names short freezes** (frames over 250 ms) and how much of them the controller update took.
