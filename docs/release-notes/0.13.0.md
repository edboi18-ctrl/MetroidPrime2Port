This is an early release. Please report what you hit and attach the log (F1 > System > Log).

**You need your own disc image:** Metroid Prime 2: Echoes USA (`G2ME01`), PAL (`G2MP01`), or Trilogy (`R3ME01` USA, `R3MP01` PAL). No disc image or game assets are included.

## What changed

- **Phazon shot anywhere** (F1 > Cheats): a full charge fires the final fight's Phazon shot. #100
- **The helmet no longer looks distorted** in widescreen and portrait: it stretches evenly, as on an emulator. #97
- **Faster rendering:** models keep their vertex data on the GPU, each vertex array is uploaded once per frame, and textures drawn with several palettes are no longer re-read on each switch. Samus, the Morph Ball and the Scan Visor keep their shaders loaded. #98
- **Save states keep more:** the Landing Site Red Eye Statue's collision and pose, and save states from before a field change no longer make scan targets combat-only. Thanks to rivyr. #90 #105
- **Resume and save-state loads stay black until Samus's room has its saved state.** Thanks to rivyr. #94
- **Steam Input (Linux):** gyro and back buttons of the controller behind Steam, Sony and Nintendo controllers keep their lights, and a controller connected to a Steam Deck takes Player 1. Quitting from Steam no longer crashes. Thanks to rivyr. #91 #92
- **Steam Deck button glyphs** for the Steam Deck and Steam Controller, one mapping file per controller, and a bound prompt icon shows on its first draw. Thanks to rivyr. #88 #89 #93
- **Cleaner button prompts:** the scan, logbook and message boxes draw their button ring sharply at any resolution. Thanks to rivyr. #102 #106
- **Screenshots save as PNG** (Screenshot binding, F12, F1 > Game > Screenshot), on Android to Pictures.
- **The Morph Ball camera follows the FOV setting** (F1 > Video; off under Original experience).
- **The F1 overlay** shows Latin Extended, Greek and Cyrillic text, and is no longer oversized on iOS and macOS.
