This is an early release. Please report what you hit and attach the log (F1 > System > Log).

**You need your own disc image:** Metroid Prime 2: Echoes USA (`G2ME01`), PAL (`G2MP01`), or Trilogy (`R3ME01` USA, `R3MP01` PAL). No disc image or game assets are included.

## What changed

- **Prime 1 jumps and dashes** (F1 > Enhancements > Prime 1 Player Physics, off by default): L-jumps, R-jumps and scan dashes as in the first game. Thanks to BubbleTea033. #122
- **Prime 1 Boost Ball** (same page, off by default): the first game's Boost Ball, with its values read from your own Prime 1 disc, and a Boost Dampening slider. Thanks to BubbleTea033. #122
- **Direct aim:** the view stays where a lock-on left it, and a custom first-person FOV is kept through world loads and cinematics. Thanks to BubbleTea033. #107
- **Mouse, twin-stick and touch aim** face the right way after an elevator cutscene. #121
- **Widescreen helmet:** it no longer swings while the camera turns. #97
- **Notes and the update notice are drawn in the game's text.** Thanks to rivyr. #119
- **Controllers:** outside a multiplayer match the pad used last plays player 1, so a saved Player 1 controller no longer blocks swapping between touch and a controller. #120
- **PAL memory cards:** a real PAL `.gci` is no longer read as corrupt. #95
- **Echo locks** in Main Research and Aerie open after reloading with some emitters already shot. #123
- **Save states:** one can no longer be loaded at the title before a save file is chosen, which left save stations not offering to save and many sounds missing. Quick resume and in-game loads are unchanged. #124
- **Hunter Ing** fade out when they die. #96
- **Phazon shot cheat:** its charge, fire and hit sounds play everywhere. #100
- **Doors above 60 fps:** the room behind an open door no longer reloads its textures over and over.
- **The left arm stays visible** when aiming steeply up or down with direct aim.
- **Fast Morph:** the zoom ends with the morph.
- **The log names freezes of 1 to 5 seconds** and where they happened.
